Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, payments, and parcel security.

How are parcel payments handled? ▼
We accept payments via M‑PESA (Buy Goods Till / Paybill) or Cash upon pickup or successful delivery. For merchant deliveries, payment on delivery (POD) reconciliation can be pre‑arranged over WhatsApp.
What size and weight limits apply to motorcycle delivery? ▼
We carry standard parcels, documents, laptops, food crates, clothing bales, and small appliances up to 25 kg that fit securely on standard motorbikes. For bulkier cargo, contact us in advance to discuss arrangement options.
How do I track my delivery in real-time? ▼
Unlike automated app platforms with lagging GPS pins, our rider communicates directly with you on WhatsApp. You receive direct status updates, pickup confirmation photos, and a notification as soon as the item reaches the recipient.
What safety protocols are followed for passenger rides? ▼
Every passenger is provided with a sanitized safety helmet and a disposable hairnet. Riders adhere to strict urban speed regulations and safe road handling.
Do you offer business accounts with invoicing? ▼
Yes! Regular business clients can open a monthly account with consolidated invoicing, priority dispatch, and negotiated volume rates. Contact us on WhatsApp to set up your business profile.
What happens if my parcel is damaged or lost? ▼
All fragile items are insured up to KSh 5,000 at no extra cost. In the rare event of damage or loss, we initiate an immediate claim process and compensate within 48 hours. High-value items can be insured for additional coverage upon request.
